Questions business owners ask us

Will my customers' details be shared?

No. In CSV files we automatically blank out columns like names, emails, phone numbers and addresses before anything is stored. You can check what's left before you publish.

What if nobody bids?

Nothing happens and you pay nothing. Listing is free — our 5% fee only applies when something actually sells.

How do I get paid?

Add your PayPal email (or ask for a bank transfer) in your account. We send your money within 5 business days of the sale.
